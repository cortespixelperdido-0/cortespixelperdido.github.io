Cortes Pixel Perdido – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Who we are. Cortes Pixel Perdido is a content creation tool operated by an individual creator in Brazil. Contact: cortespixelperdido@gmail.com

2. Data we collect from TikTok. When you connect your TikTok account, we receive only what you authorize:

• basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar);

• access tokens needed to upload videos on your behalf;

• statistics of videos published by the account (views, likes, comments and shares counts).

3. How we use it. Only to upload videos to the connected account and to measure the performance of those videos so future content can be improved. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties for advertising.

4. Storage and security. Tokens and data are stored on our private server, protected by access control, and are never exposed publicly.

5. Retention and deletion. Data is kept while the account remains connected. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions), and you can request deletion of all stored data by emailing cortespixelperdido@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.

6. Third-party services. To produce videos we use services such as AI text and voice generation and stock media libraries. Your TikTok personal data is not sent to these services.

7. Your rights (LGPD). Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei 13.709/2018), you may request access, correction or deletion of your data through the contact email above.

8. Changes. This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
